How RapidAid Coordinates Emergencies

Seamless automated workflow bridging patients, dispatchers, ambulance fleets, and emergency centers.

1. Emergency Reported

Patient details and critical emergency location are logged into the system immediately by staff.

2. Request Logged

The request enters the active dispatch queue as PENDING with real-time priority markers.

3. Resource Assigned

System pairs an available ambulance with a destination hospital, auto-updating unit statuses and bed counts.

4. Patient Transported

Upon successful delivery, the request is marked COMPLETED and the ambulance is restored to service.
