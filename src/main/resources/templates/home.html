How RapidAid Coordinates Emergency Dispatches

From public emergency requests to AI priority scoring, nearest-unit matching, and live map tracking.

1
Public Request

Patient submits pickup coordinates via GPS without requiring a login.

2
Priority Scoring

Rule-based AI engine scores emergency urgency (HIGH / MED / LOW).

3
Nearest Match

Computes Haversine distance to suggest the closest available unit.

4
Live Map Track

Driver streams position over WebSocket STOMP for live map tracking.

5
Completion

Hospital receives alert, patient arrives, and resources reset.

No! Anyone can request immediate emergency assistance via our public request portal at /request. You can also use your browser's GPS to automatically populate your pickup coordinates.

Assigned drivers stream real-time location telemetry from their mobile browser. Location updates are broadcast over Spring WebSocket STOMP directly to your live tracking map at /request/track/{id}.

Our PriorityEngine evaluates emergency symptom descriptions (e.g., cardiac arrest, stroke, trauma) to assign a numeric urgency score and color-coded priority badge (HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW) so critical cases are dispatched first.

RapidAid integrates with Twilio for SMS and Jakarta SMTP for Emails. Drivers receive instant dispatch details, hospitals get incoming transfer alerts, and requesters receive confirmation updates. If credentials are missing, system operates gracefully with audit logging.
